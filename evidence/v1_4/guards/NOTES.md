# The optimized build against the conservative one

`differential.json` is one run of `tools/checks/differential_guards.py --programs 2000 --batch 50 --workers 3 --gcc --output evidence/v1_4/guards/differential.json` at commit 9087d5c, on a sixteen-thread x86-64 WSL2 machine that other agents' builds and suites shared at the time. Sharing slows the run and changes nothing it compares.

The harness generated 2000 functions of the shape `gN(n, m, k, x:ro<u64>[n], y:ro<u64>[m]) -> u64` from statements that stress what guard elision reasons about: loops with early returns and branches, conditions joined by `&&` and `||`, immutable `let`s, an owner replaced by assignment, narrowing conversions, parts whose extent is left out or written as `hi - lo`, with and without a test that settles them, and arithmetic next to the largest usize. The checker refused 179 of them, 160 with `E-UNBOUND` for a loop index named outside its loop and 19 with `E-SHADOW` for an owner bound twice, and those were dropped. The other 1821 were compiled twice: as the compiler emits them, where the 4015 guards the checker discharged and `verify/elision.py` accepted are left out and calls reach the lean bodies of their callees, and with `keep_guards`, which writes every guard and sends every call through the callee's checked entry.

Both builds ran under clang++ with AddressSanitizer and UndefinedBehaviorSanitizer and under g++ without sanitizers, at `-O1`. A driver called every function on 96 inputs, every `n` and `m` in 0, 1, 2 and 5 and every `k` in 0, 1, 3, 7 and the two largest usize values, each in its own process, and printed the returned value, `trap` for an abort, or the exit status of anything else, which is how a sanitizer report or a hang would show. That is 174,816 cases per compiler. The two builds printed the same line for every case under both compilers: no value, no trap and no status differed, so there was no counterexample to minimize.

What this establishes is finite-tested agreement on these generated programs and inputs. It says nothing about shapes the generator does not write, such as lanes, records, or loops over `k`, which it avoids so that no case runs for 2^64 iterations. `tests/soundness/test_elision.py` runs eight generated functions of the same harness on every suite run, and checks that the harness reports a difference when every index guard is dropped behind the audit's back.
