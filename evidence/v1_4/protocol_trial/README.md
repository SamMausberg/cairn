# Edit-protocol trial: before any subject

The trial is designed in `tools/ai/protocol_trial.md` and has not run. This folder holds what was checked before it may.

`verify.json` is `python3 tools/ai/protocol_trial.py verify`: for each of the twelve tasks, the program as shipped passes its hidden check and the planted program fails it. `rehearsal.json` is `prepare`, `rehearse` and `score` over every task and both arms with a scripted subject that sends the shipped body, and under the focused arm first expands what that body calls that the packet does not show, or else the first callee written in the program. It solves twelve of twelve in each arm, which shows that the harness and every task work through the host.

The scripted subject read 121,128 `o200k_base` tokens from the component host and 29,813 from the focused one, over 24 and 27 calls. That is the protocol's floor with no model in it: one expansion, one edit, a submit. It says nothing yet about what a model spends.
